esp-hci-bridge

Olimex ESP32-POE as a remote Bluetooth controller over Ethernet.

Firmware …, built ….

Needs Chromium, Chrome or Edge. Firefox has no Web Serial. Open this page over HTTPS or localhost.

Plug the board's micro USB into this computer, click Install, pick the CH340 port. The dialog also has a serial console for watching boot logs.

Non ISO ESP32-POE: unplug PoE before connecting USB. Olimex warns the missing isolation can damage the PC.

Linux: your user needs access to /dev/ttyUSB0, usually group dialout or uucp. Flatpak browsers need --device=all.

After flashing, on the PC:

modprobe hci_vhci
hcibridged --host esp-hci-bridge
bluetoothctl list
